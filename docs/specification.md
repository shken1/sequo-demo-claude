# Specification

## What the product is

A web-based habit tracker for two equal partners. It maintains one shared streak that both partners can see. Either partner can log the current day to extend the shared streak. The product consists of a single page that shows the current streak length and provides a button to log today.

## Target audience

Two people who want to build a habit together and hold each other accountable. They need a simple way to both see the same streak count and to let either of them mark a day as complete without friction or separate accounts.

## Core value proposition

Both partners instantly see the same live streak number. Either one can press a single button to log the current day and increment the shared streak, keeping motivation high through immediate shared visibility and equal contribution.

## How it works

1. A partner opens the web app in their browser and sees the current shared streak length displayed prominently.
2. When either partner completes the habit for the day, they press the "Log Today" button.
3. The streak length increases by one and the new value appears immediately on both partners' screens.

## Out of scope for MVP

- Individual streaks or private data for each partner
- History of past logged days
- Any additional features, analytics, or customization
